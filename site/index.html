How it works

Three steps. No POS integration. Just Claude.

1

Upload your history

Paste your existing daily sales spreadsheet into Claude. PrepCast reads any format — CSV, tab-separated, or even just date/amount lines.

2

Tell it about today

Just say it in plain English — "volleyball tournament, 6,000 people." PrepCast factors in day-of-week baselines, recent trends, and event multipliers.

3

Get your prep list

Every item with exact quantities and case counts. Log your actuals at end of day and the model gets more accurate over time.

14 tools, one goal

Each tool does exactly one thing. Ask Claude to run any of them.

Sales Data
upload_sales_csv Paste your spreadsheet. Auto-detects date + revenue columns.
log_daily_sales Log today's actual revenue at end of shift.
get_sales_summary How much history is loaded, date range, daily averages.
Forecasting
forecast_sales Project revenue for any date with optional event adjustment.
analyze_history Day-of-week patterns, best/worst days, trend chart.
Prep Lists
generate_prep_list Full item quantities from projected revenue + safety buffer.
update_menu_ratios Tune patty case size, bacon split %, etc. to your store.
get_menu_ratios Review and calibrate current prep ratios.
Events
get_upcoming_events Fetch nearby venue events with estimated traffic multipliers.
log_event_outcome Record actual sales after event days. Model learns your store.
get_event_multipliers See learned multipliers — volleyball vs cornhole vs soccer.
Works with Claude.ai

Connect in 60 seconds

PrepCast is already live. Add it to Claude with one URL.

1

Go to Claude.ai → Settings → Integrations

Live now

Click Add Integration and paste this URL:

https://prepcast-mcp.onrender.com/sse

That's it. No install, no config file, no code.

2

Start a new chat in Claude

PrepCast tools will appear in the tools panel. Just talk to Claude normally:

"We have a volleyball tournament Saturday, about 6,000 people. What should I prep?"
3

Upload your sales history

Tell Claude: "I want to upload my sales history" and paste your spreadsheet. After 30+ days of data, forecasts become accurate to your specific location.

First 3 things to say to Claude

  1. 1."Show me my current prep ratios" — review defaults, adjust to your store
  2. 2."I want to upload my sales history" — paste your CSV when prompted
  3. 3."What's my prep list for tomorrow? Soccer tournament, 3,000 people."
